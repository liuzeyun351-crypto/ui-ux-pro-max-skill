03 — How the call gets made

Nobody enjoys
cancelling football.

But a field played on when it is saturated takes six weeks to recover, and the teams who lose those six weeks are the MiniRoos. So the rule is boring and it does not bend.

Read the wet weather policy
  1. 1

    3:30pm weekdays. The ground manager walks all four surfaces and checks goalmouths, centre circles and the training grid.

  2. 2

    4:30pm weekdays. Status is published on this page and sent to coaches. No status change means no change.

  3. 3

    6:30am Saturday. Weekend call is published here and on the club's social accounts before anyone leaves home.

  4. 4

    Council override. Brisbane City Council can close a ground independently. If they do, we cannot overrule it.

  5. 5

    Referee override. Once a match starts, the referee owns the decision to stop or abandon it.
