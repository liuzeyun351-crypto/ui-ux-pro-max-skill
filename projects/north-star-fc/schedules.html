Note 01

Arrive fifteen early

Boots on and warm-up started at the listed time, not arriving at it. Sessions finish on the minute because the next squad is waiting.

Note 02

Lights out at 9:30pm

Field lighting is on a council timer. Senior sessions finishing at 8:30pm leave an hour for pack-up and the shed.

Note 03

Bring water, always

There is a refill station beside the canteen. In summer, coaches enforce breaks every twenty minutes above 31°C.

03 — Match day

How a Saturday runs.

07:30

Canteen opens

Coffee, bacon and egg rolls, and the volunteer roster sheet on the counter. Cash and card both work.

08:00

First whistle

MiniRoos start the day on Field 1, rotating through grids. Parents are welcome on the sideline, not the field.

13:00

Juniors and seniors

The competitive grades take over Field 1 for the afternoon. Ground marshals in yellow can answer anything.

19:00

Pack down

Nets, corner flags, rubbish, lights. Every team takes a turn across the season — it is on the roster.

Parking and getting here

The main car park fills by 9am on a full Saturday. Overflow parking is on the eastern side of the complex and it is a two-minute walk. Do not park on the grass verges — that is where the council starts asking questions.

Accessibility

Accessible parking is beside the clubhouse with a sealed path to Field 1 and the accessible toilet. Field 2 is reachable on a compacted path. If you need a different arrangement for a match day, email us and we will sort it out.

Times change. We will tell you when.

Fixture changes, training moves and closures all go out by email to registered players and coaches. This page is updated at the same time, so it is always the source of truth.
