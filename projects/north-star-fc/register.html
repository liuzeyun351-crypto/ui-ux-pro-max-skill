01 — How it works

Three steps.

Step 01

Register on PlayFootball

Every player in Australia registers through PlayFootball, the national system. Choose North Star Football Club and the age group that matches the player's birth year — not their school year.

About 10 minutesRequired
Step 02

Apply your FairPlay voucher

Queensland Government FairPlay vouchers of up to $200 are applied at checkout. Apply for the voucher first — you need the code before you pay, and it cannot be added afterwards.

Before you payUp to $200
Step 03

Tell us your preference

Once you are paid, email the registrar with the player's name, age group and any squad, coach or friend request. We grade in the two weeks before the season and place as many requests as we can.

Same weekOne email

The season,
in order.

Registration closes when squads are full, and popular age groups fill before the closing date. Late registrations go on a waitlist and are placed if a team has room.

05 — Common questions

Before you email us.

What age group is my child in?

Age groups run on birth year, not school year. A player turning nine at any point in 2026 plays in the Under 9s. If you are on a cusp, email us the birth date.

Can my child play with a friend?

In MiniRoos, almost always — put both names in your email. From Under 12 squads are graded by ability, so we place requests where we can but cannot promise.

What do we need to buy?

Your playing shirt is included. You supply shorts, socks, shin pads and boots. The club runs a second-hand boot swap in February — bring outgrown pairs.

Do you take beginners at any age?

Yes, including adults. Senior division four and over-35s take players who have never played. Tell us it is your first season and we will place you accordingly.

Can I get a refund?

Before the first game, yes, less the governing body levies we have already paid. After the season starts it is pro-rata for injury. See the refund policy.

Do you need volunteers?

Always. Coaching, managing, canteen, ground marking, the barbecue. No experience needed and the club pays for coaching courses and blue cards.

Still not sure where you fit?

Tell us the player's age and how much football they have played, and the registrar will tell you which program to pick. Nobody gets talked into the wrong squad.
