01 — What you are buying

A local audience
that actually turns up.

640 registered players means roughly 1,900 people through the gates across a season — parents, grandparents, siblings and opposition supporters, most of them living within six kilometres of your front door.

These are counted from registrations and gate attendance, not estimated from social media impressions. If a number here looks small, it is because it is real.

03 — Where it goes

No sponsor money
goes to salaries.

Nobody at this club is paid. Sponsorship covers the things registration fees cannot stretch to, and the committee publishes exactly where it went in the annual report every February.

Read the annual report
  1. $18k

    Field lighting and irrigation across three surfaces, every season.

  2. $9k

    Coach accreditation courses and blue card processing for volunteers.

  3. $7k

    Fee assistance beyond FairPlay, so no child sits out a season over money.

  4. $6k

    Balls, nets, bibs, cones, goals and the All Abilities equipment.
