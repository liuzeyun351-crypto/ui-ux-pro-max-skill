03 — Since 1974

A club built by
people who stayed.

North Star started with two teams, a borrowed set of goals and a canteen run out of the boot of a Kingswood. Fifty-two years later the canteen has walls, but the arrangement has not really changed: parents run it, players come back to coach, and nobody gets paid to care.

We are an incorporated not-for-profit association. Every dollar of registration income goes back into referees, equipment, coach accreditation and the grounds. Our accounts are published each February on the documents page.

  1. 1974

    Founded by a group of families on the northside. Two junior teams, one set of borrowed goals.

  2. 1983

    First senior side enters the Brisbane competition. Clubhouse built by members over two winters.

  3. 1996

    Women's football starts at the club. Two teams in the first season, six by the third.

  4. 2008

    Field 2 and Field 3 added after a council partnership. Registrations pass 400 for the first time.

  5. 2017

    MiniRoos program restructured around equal game time and rotating positions.

  6. 2022

    Admitted to the Football Queensland Premier League pathway with junior and senior squads.

  7. 2026

    640 registered players, 46 teams, and an All Abilities program in its fourth season.
