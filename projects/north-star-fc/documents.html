publish it.

Members are entitled to see how the club is run. If a document you need is not here, email the secretary and it will either be sent to you or added to this page — usually within a week.

Complaints follow the grievance procedure and go to the Member Protection Information Officer, not to a coach or a committee member you happen to know.
